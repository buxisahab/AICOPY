---
description: Verify the mandatory username creation flow
---

1. Build and install the app.
2. Ensure you are logged out, or clear app data.
3. Login using Google Sign-In.
4. When the Username Creation screen appears, minimize the app or kill it from the recent apps list.
5. Re-open the app.
6. Verify that you are redirected back to the Username Creation screen, and NOT to the Home screen with a random username.
7. Enter a valid username and proceed.
8. Verify that you can now access the Home screen.
